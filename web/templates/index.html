Questions

Good to know

Why does the bot need a reply?

X's API exposes the parent post through the reply reference. A standalone mention does not tell the bot which GIF you want.

What if the post has a video instead of a GIF?

Videos work too. The download page always offers the original MP4, and the conversion endpoint can turn short videos into GIFs.

Why is the GIF larger than the MP4?

GIF is an old, inefficient format. The converter limits width and frame rate for a sensible balance between quality and file size.

Does this work with protected or deleted posts?

No. The bot only works with public posts whose media is still available from X's CDN.
